Big ideas to remember

Useful context for building a stronger analytical paragraph.

Jacobean beliefs

Many people believed the king was chosen by God. Challenging the monarch was seen as a sin against the natural and divine order.

Great Chain of Being

A hierarchy believed to organise all creation, with God at the top, then the monarch, people, animals and plants. Regicide disrupts this order.

Gender and power

Lady Macbeth rejects expectations of femininity, while the play questions whether cruelty and violence are signs of strength.
